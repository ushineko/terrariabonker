# Validation Report — the sell log's price

**Date**: 2026-10-13 13:45
**Commit**: uncommitted (working tree on `main` at f4c873e); released as v0.45.2
**Status**: PASSED headless; prices checked against the running game by dry run.
**Scope**: `internal/gui/views_effects.go` and a new test. The window's log
only. Nothing that reads or writes the game.

## What was wrong

The window's log said `sold <item> x<n> for <nil>` under every sale. The line
read a field named `value` from the sale reply; the reply's field is `copper`.
The sale itself was unaffected: the price is computed and the coins paid in
the service, which the window does not take part in.

## What changed

- The log reads `copper` and writes it as coins: `sold Nebula Fragment x45 for
  9 gold`. A stack worth nothing reads `for nothing`.
- Version 0.45.1 to 0.45.2.

## Prices, against the running game (Windows 11, 1.4.5.8)

`sell --dry-run --json --force`, with `APPDATA` pointed at an empty temporary
folder holding a list of every carried item type, so the maintainer's list was
not touched and nothing was sold. Twelve unfavorited stacks were priced, among
them Recall Potion 200, Greater Healing Potion x6 6000, Ancient Manipulator
50000, Nebula Fragment x45 90000, Mushroom 250, Chlorophyte Bullet x867 8670,
Gel x60 60 (copper). Each is the game's sell price times the stack.
