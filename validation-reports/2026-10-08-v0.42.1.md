# Validation Report — terrariabonker v0.42.1

**Date**: 2026-10-08
**Scope**: A patch release for Linux. Since v0.42.0: three player-handling fixes, two
dependency updates, and the native-Windows work of spec 052 (phases 0–3, still in
progress; no Windows artifact is built or released).

## What a Linux player gets

- **A buffed player is found.** With the cap in effect above the permanent life cap
  (Lifeforce, max-life accessories), the live player failed validation and was not
  found, so cheats, edits and restores acted on other copies or failed. Two causes,
  both fixed: the life caps were named the wrong way round
  (`2026-10-08-life-caps-named-by-the-runtime.md`), and the scan's prefilter bounded
  life by the permanent cap (`2026-10-08-buffed-player-scan.md`). Shown on the live
  game: with one copy put in the buffed state, v0.42.0 found 1 copy, this release 2.
- **Mana fills to the cap in effect.** Mana-to-full and the mana freeze stopped at the
  permanent cap, short under a mana boost.
- **Writes reach only the character being played.** With copies of several characters
  in memory and no ground truth, a write is refused instead of reaching all of them
  (`2026-10-08-spec-052-phase-3e.md`). Normal Linux play -- one character's copies --
  is unchanged.
- Dependencies: fynedesygn v0.1.87; `golang.org/x/net` v0.60.0 (GO-2026-6610..6617,
  not called by this code).

The component reports for each commit are in this directory, dated 2026-10-08.
