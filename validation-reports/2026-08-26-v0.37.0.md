# Validation Report — terrariabonker v0.37.0

**Date**: 2026-08-26
**Scope**: The arena bootstrap's own springboard (v0.36.0's largest known gap), and the
three fishing potions' effects without the potions (spec 044).

## Summary

A small release with one fix that matters more than the feature. v0.36.0 shipped knowing
that the arena bootstrap could not run while the ore extractor was enabled; that is now
fixed and confirmed against the case that was broken.

The feature is passive potions pointed at three specific buffs, with the potion
requirement removed — and the interesting part of it is a precedence rule the maintainer
asked for that turned out to be already true, and a report line that was already false.

## The part worth reading

**The bootstrap has its own springboard.** `Patcher.arena()` makes the game call
`VirtualAlloc` by hanging a temporary jump on a per-frame site, and that site was
`ore_extract`'s own injection point. With the extractor enabled the site holds a jump,
`_check_site` refuses — correctly — and no arena can be allocated at all. Latent for
months, exposed by the `TBARENA2` stamp forcing a fresh allocation into a session whose
cheats had been restored on launch.

There is now a candidate list. The first entry is inside the `borders_movement` anchor at
`+0x12`, past auto-use's displaced bytes, at the instruction auto-use's own stub jumps back
to: a per-frame site belonging to no injection, whose two instructions carry no relative
address. Confirmed live with the extractor and auto-use both enabled — fresh arena
allocated, stamped and mapped, game healthy.

**A precedence rule that was already true, pinned anyway.** The maintainer's requirement
for the new fishing effects was that a real potion, and the passive-potions cheat, beat
them. That falls out of `Buffs.renew` refusing to shorten a buff — an eight-minute Fishing
Potion is left alone rather than cut to the two seconds this renews on. Inherited
behaviour is exactly what stops being true after a refactor, so it is pinned by its own
test and verified live: 28800 ticks untouched across three rounds.

**The reporting was wrong on its first live run.** `renew` returns `kept` in two different
situations — something else owns the buff, and our own renewal from a second ago has not
expired — and both were reported as "a potion is already running it". A second round a
moment after the first announced that a potion was running Sonar when the only thing
running it was the round before. Now only a buff running longer than this cheat would ever
set counts as somebody else's.

**Buff ids were read, not guessed**: ContentSamples item templates for 2354/2355/2356
carry `buffType` 121/122/123.

from 529 at v0.36.0.

## In-game verification

On 1.4.5.8+24893155:

- **All three effects held**: buffs 121, 122 and 123 added and renewed, alongside buffs the
  player already had.
- **Precedence**: a Fishing Potion buff at 28800 ticks was untouched across three rounds
  and reported as deferred, while Sonar and Crate carried on being held.
- **Springboard**: with `ore_extract` and `auto_use` both enabled, a fresh arena was
  allocated at `0x68020000`, stamped and mapped, and the cheats kept working afterwards.

## Security review (Phase 5)

- No `eval`/`exec`, no `subprocess`, no sockets, no network calls in shipped code.
- No hardcoded credentials.
- Dependencies unchanged since v0.34.0, so `pip-audit` was not re-run.
- The new writes are two ints per effect in the player's own buff arrays. Nothing is
  written to the world, the save, or to disk.
- The springboard change moves *where* a temporary jump is written during bootstrap; it is
  still restored in a `finally`, and the site is still checked before being written.

## Release safety

- **Rollback**: `git revert` or the previous tag. The effects lapse on their own a couple
  of seconds after the last renewal — there is nothing to switch off and nothing to restore.
- The springboard keeps the extractor's old site as a fallback, so a build where
  `borders_movement` does not resolve behaves as it did before this release.

## Known gaps

- **`RELEASE_ITEM_OFF` (`Player+0x67F`) is still not identified.** Auto-use writes it and
  casting works without it; it is a byte with the right shape and nothing more.
- **The auto-use hook's call target is identified by shape and position**, not by mono
  metadata.
- **Whether switching items destroys an outstanding bobber was never settled.** It bears on
  a rejected design (gating the fishing effects on holding a rod) and on nothing shipped.
